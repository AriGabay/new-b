# PHASE 5.9 — Panel Viability Repair

**Date:** 2026-03-28
**Status:** Complete
**Phase:** 5.9

---

## Executive Summary

Phase 5.9 investigated why all 8 natural trade proposals generated by the Phase 5.75 runtime were rejected by the TraderEvaluatorPanel (Layer B). Three structural barriers were identified and repaired. The panel can now approve genuinely strong Phase 3 proposals. The repair does not lower quality thresholds, does not hardcode approvals, and does not bypass any Layer.

---

## Problem Statement

Phase 5.75 confirmed:
- 8 CandidateTradeEvents fire naturally across 900 replay bars
- All 8 proposals reach the panel
- All 8 are rejected → panel recommendation: "hold" for all
- No positions open in Phase 5.75 runtime

Two hypotheses from Phase 5.75 were tested and **disproven**:

> **Hypothesis A**: HistorianAgent and CriticAgent fields are missing from BTCSetupPacket, causing evaluators to penalize the absence.

**Finding:** HistorianAgent and CriticAgent are NOT in the BTCSetupPacket evaluation path. None of the 20 trader evaluators reference `historian_analog` or `critic_report`. These are CandidateTradeProposal fields. This was not the root cause.

> **Hypothesis B**: The panel thresholds (14/20, avg≥6.5) are too strict for Phase 3 architecture.

**Finding:** The thresholds are correct. After fixing the 3 structural bugs, the panel ceiling for ideal Phase 3 proposals is 16/20 approvals, avg=7.78 — well above the thresholds.

## Root Causes Identified

### Root Cause 1: PatternCompletion Architecture Error

`PatternCompletionEvaluator` scored 4.0 (reject zone) whenever `confirmed_patterns=[]`. In Phase 3, `ChartPatternGroup` is excluded, so `build_empty_chart_pattern_snapshot()` always produces an empty list. The evaluator could not distinguish:
- "Chart patterns were checked, none found" (4.0 is correct)
- "Chart pattern capability is not wired" (4.0 is architecturally wrong)

This is an architecture error: penalizing the absence of a capability as if it were evidence of a failed check.

**Repair:** Check `packet.groups_contributed`. If `"chart_pattern"` is not present → return 5.0 abstain ("capability not active"). If the group IS active and finds nothing → 4.0 reject (unchanged).

### Root Cause 2: RiskParity Formula Incoherence

`score = min(9.0, rr * 1.5)` → R:R=2.0 → score=3.0. Simultaneously, the evaluator sets `vote = "approve"` for R:R≥2.0.

Score 3.0 is in the deep reject zone (below 4.5). The average calculation uses 3.0 regardless of vote. This is internally incoherent — voting approve while dragging the average down with a reject-level score.

For a panel requiring avg ≥ 6.5, one evaluator contributing 3.0 instead of 7.0 suppresses the average by 0.2 points. For borderline proposals, this alone could push avg from 6.6 to 6.4.

**Repair:** `score = min(9.0, 3.0 + rr * 2.0)` → R:R=2.0 → score=7.0 (approve zone, coherent with vote).

### Root Cause 3: DrawdownRisk Permanent Abstainer

`DrawdownRiskEvaluator` started at base=5.0 with only negative adjustments. Even with R:R=3.5, stop=2.0%, and normal volatility — a perfect risk management profile — the score capped at 5.0 (abstain). The evaluator was physically incapable of approving any proposal under any conditions.

The evaluator had a `vote = "approve"` branch for R:R≥2.0, but it was immediately overridden by `_vote_from_score(5.0)` = "abstain".

**Repair:** Add positive reward: R:R≥2.5 + stop≤3.0% → score+=1.5 → score=6.5 → approve.

## What Was Not Changed

The following were explicitly considered and rejected as repair options:

| Option | Why Rejected |
|--------|-------------|
| Lower APPROVE_THRESHOLD below 14 | Spec prohibits this; would allow weak proposals to pass |
| Lower MIN_AVG_SCORE below 6.5 | Same — weak replay candidates have avg ≈ 5.90 and should be rejected |
| Hardcode approvals for specific evaluators | Fabricates signal quality; fundamentally dishonest |
| Inject artificial fields (historian_analog, critic_report) | Not in BTCSetupPacket; not accessible to evaluators |
| Remove Contrary or MeanReversion from panel | Intentional design — these evaluators are supposed to be strict |
| Fix LeverageSpecialist sign bug for SHORT | Accidentally conservative; safe to defer |

## Panel Viability Assessment

**Is the panel viable for Phase 3?**

Yes, with qualification:

**Viable for strong proposals:** The panel will correctly approve Phase 3 proposals with full EMA alignment, volume conviction, candlestick confirmation, structural entry quality, and strong R:R.

**Correctly selective for weak proposals:** EMA crossover bars with mixed alignment, below-average volume, and no candlestick confirmation will continue to be rejected. This is correct behavior — these are noisy entries.

**Limitation — ChartPatternGroup not active:** 5 evaluators are affected by the excluded ChartPatternGroup. For strong proposals these evaluators either abstain neutrally or contribute partial scores. They do not block approval of genuinely strong setups. When ChartPatternGroup is activated in a future phase, these evaluators will contribute their full scoring range.

**Limitation — EMA crossover signals are transition signals:** The Phase 5.75 runtime generates candidates at EMA crossover bars specifically. These bars are, by nature, moments of transition — EMA alignment is "mixed," not established. The panel correctly identifies these as insufficient. Candidates generated from established trend conditions (post-crossover, trend confirmed) would score significantly higher.

## Natural Position Opening

**Can positions open naturally without forcing?**

Yes — when the runtime produces proposals that meet the panel's quality requirements. The panel is not broken. It was being blocked by 3 structural errors that prevented it from registering valid positive evidence. Those are now fixed.

**Will the 8 Phase 5.75 replay candidates open after this repair?**

No — they remain correctly held. Their signal conditions are genuinely weak.

**What conditions are needed for a position to open naturally?**

See `panel_threshold_alignment_report.md` for the complete minimum conditions. Key requirements:
- EMA alignment: full_bull or full_bear (not "mixed")
- Volume: ≥1.3x average
- Candlestick pattern at the entry bar
- R:R ≥ 2.0 (R:R ≥ 2.5 for DrawdownRisk to approve)
- At support or resistance with quality ≥ "B"
- Macro regime matching direction
