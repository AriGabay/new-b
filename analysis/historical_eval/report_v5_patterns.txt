4. KEY FINDING: CHART PATTERN DIRECTION CONFLICT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  When chart pattern signals (e.g., Diamond Top SHORT) were included
  in direction voting alongside indicator signals (LONG), they caused
  direction conflicts that suppressed proposals. Trade count dropped
  from 58 → 34 with chart patterns included in voting.

  FIX: Chart pattern signals are now EXCLUDED from direction voting
  in EntryGroup._evaluate_trade_opportunity(). They still contribute
  to has_bar_level_confirmation and composite score bonus, but their
  direction does not participate in the majority vote.

7. STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The system is PROFITABLE with 60.3% WR, PF 1.21, and 15% max DD.
  The main gap is trade frequency (0.5/week) and absolute PnL (+$95).

  Root cause: With $1,000 equity, 5% max risk per trade, and daily
  loss limit at -15%, the system can only sustain ~3 losses per day.
  The 15-approval panel threshold ensures high quality but limits
  how many proposals pass.

  The 53-pattern detector suite provides rich signal diversity, but
  on single-symbol 1h BTC, the market structure only produces a few
  high-quality setups per week that get 15/20 evaluator approvals.

  Path to 3+ trades/week: multi-symbol trading (ETH, SOL, etc.)
  would multiply opportunities without lowering quality threshold.
