7. REMAINING GAP: TRADE FREQUENCY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Current: 1.3 trades/week (141 in 110 weeks)
  Target:  3.0 trades/week (330 in 110 weeks)
  Gap:     2.3x more trades needed

  Root cause: Panel at T=14 approves only 151 of 9,191 proposals (1.6%).
  The panel evaluators (20 deterministic traders) are highly selective at
  T=14 because they require strong EMA alignment + directional confirmation.
  Lowering to T=13 increases trades but WR drops to 43% (unprofitable).

  Structural limit: The panel is the quality gate. Below T=14, proposals
  lack sufficient confirmation diversity and lose money. Above T=14,
  too few proposals pass.

  The system is profitable at 1.3 trades/week: +51.7% return, PF 1.41,
  61.7% WR. The trade frequency target of 3/week would require either
  (a) fundamentally different signal types that the panel evaluators
  approve at T=14, or (b) multi-symbol trading across BTC, ETH, SOL etc.
