4. ROOT CAUSE OF FAILURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [11B] REGIME THRESHOLD T=12 IS BELOW THE PROFITABLE RANGE

  V5 threshold sweep (fixed T, same data, same pipeline) showed:
    T=12:  n/a (not explicitly tested, but inferred from T=13)
    T=13:  63 trades  49.2% WR  PF 0.54  DD 38.6%  PnL $-345
    T=14:  34 trades  35.3% WR  PF 0.36  DD 39.4%  PnL $-388
    T=15:  58 trades  60.3% WR  PF 1.21  DD 15.0%  PnL $+95  ← ONLY PROFITABLE

  Task 11B set bull/trending = T=12. BTC spent a large portion of
  2024–2025 in "bull" or "trending" regime. With T=12 applied for
  most of the backtest, the system mirrors the T=13 row — high
  frequency but below-breakeven quality.

  The V5 sweep proved T=15 is the UNIQUE structural optimum. Below
  T=15, the panel lets through too many marginal proposals.

  Win rate collapsed: 60.3% → 46.1%. At 46.1% WR with 1:1 R:R,
  the expected PF ≈ 0.85 (actual: 0.86). Mathematically correct
  given the inputs — the threshold calibration is the root cause.
